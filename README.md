# 📊 PwC Customer Churn Analysis | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on **Customer Churn Analysis using Microsoft Power BI**, inspired by PwC's Customer Retention case study.

The objective is to analyze customer behavior, identify churn patterns, understand the factors contributing to customer attrition, and provide actionable insights that can help businesses improve customer retention.

The interactive dashboard transforms customer data into meaningful visualizations and key performance indicators (KPIs) to support data-driven decision-making.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze customer churn rates and retention patterns.
* Identify customer segments with higher churn.
* Understand the relationship between customer demographics, services, contracts, and churn.
* Evaluate customer tenure and billing patterns.
* Develop interactive dashboards to communicate key business insights.
* Recommend strategies to improve customer retention.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **Microsoft Power BI** — Dashboard development and visualization
* **Power Query** — Data cleaning and transformation
* **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** — Measures and KPI calculations
* **Data Analytics** — Customer segmentation and churn analysis

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard can be used to analyze the following metrics, depending on the measures included in the report:

* **Total Customers** — Total number of customers in the dataset.
* **Churned Customers** — Number of customers who discontinued the service.
* **Churn Rate (%)** — Percentage of customers who discontinued the service.
* **Retained Customers** — Number of customers who continued using the service.
* **Average Tenure** — Average length of the customer relationship.
* **Monthly Charges** — Monthly service charges across customer segments.

## 🔍 Key Areas of Analysis

### 1. Customer Churn Overview

Analyze the overall customer base, churn rate, and retention performance.

### 2. Customer Demographics

Explore churn patterns across demographic groups, such as gender and senior-citizen status, where available.

### 3. Contract Analysis

Examine the relationship between contract types and customer churn.

### 4. Services and Subscriptions

Investigate whether subscribed services and service combinations are associated with different churn patterns.

### 5. Tenure and Billing Analysis

Explore how customer tenure, monthly charges, and payment methods relate to churn.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the patterns identified in the dashboard, potential retention strategies include:

* Identify high-risk customer segments and prioritize retention efforts.
* Investigate why customers on short-term contracts may be more likely to leave.
* Evaluate targeted retention offers for customers with high monthly charges.
* Improve customer onboarding and engagement during the early stages of the customer lifecycle.
* Monitor churn-related KPIs regularly to evaluate retention initiatives.

*These are potential recommendations. Actual conclusions should be based on the results shown in the dashboard.*
